PoloIQ™

Founder · Est. 2026 · Patent pending

Voice-powered stats and real-time coaching insights for water polo

PoloIQ™ is software for coaches, managers, and statisticians. Track every stat hands-free by voice, synced to the official game clock, then get explainable, data-driven recommendations for lineups, matchups, and plays during the game.

🎙️ "dark 3 excluded by light 2" → Exclusion: Dark #3 vs #2 · Exclusion drawn: Light #2 vs #3 · Q3 4:12
Inquire about PoloIQ™ See the product
🗣️

Hands-free voice stats

Call out plays the way coaches already talk. PoloIQ™ resolves "dark" and "light" caps to the right team for that game, understands active and passive voice, and logs linked events for both players: who was excluded, who drew it, who assisted, who blocked whom. Say "undo" to take it back.

⏱️

Camera reads the scoreboard

Point a phone at the scoreboard and draw a box around the clock once. PoloIQ™ reads the digits live and re-syncs after every whistle, timeout, and stoppage, so every event lands on the official game clock, not the phone's.

📝

Speak your own stats

Name a player and say anything ("too high in the lane," "great center D") and it becomes a coaching note on that player. Notes a coach keeps repeating turn into measurable trends the engine uses.

🤝

Head-to-head ledger

Every player-vs-player result is tracked across every meeting: goals scored on, exclusions drawn, steals, blocks, sprints won. Each player's timeline shows every event with the period, game clock, score, and the opponent involved.

🧠

Explainable tactics engine

Set up the situation on a drag-and-drop tactics board. PoloIQ™ tests who performs above or below their norm in this exact situation (period, score, man-up or man-down) for statistical significance, scores every matchup, and recommends a substitution, matchup, and play. Each call comes with ranked, plain-English reasons and a confidence level.

📈

Gets smarter every game

One tap answers "Did it work?" PoloIQ™ tracks which plays actually succeed for your team in each situation and re-ranks them, without letting a lucky streak take over. Every signal is recomputed as new games are logged by voice or imported.

📊

Scouting & game plans, automated

Box scores and live-scoring feeds are pulled in daily and merged into one timeline. Before each game, PoloIQ™ builds a plan: the record against the opponent, the last meeting, who has scored against them, and room for staff notes.

🏀

Built for water polo, ready for any sport

The same system carries over to basketball, hockey, soccer, lacrosse, volleyball, football, baseball, and more. Only the vocabulary changes.
